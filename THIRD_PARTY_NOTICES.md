Photoscanned materials, props and HDRI skies from Poly Haven
(https://polyhaven.com/license) and ambientCG (https://ambientcg.com), CC0 1.0.
Each asset's authors, files and source page are listed in
content/manifest/maps.json, content/manifest/maps/<map>.json (Harbor and Village)
and content/licenses/polyhaven-environment.txt. Harbor and Village add Poly Haven
skies, textures and props, and ambientCG decals and foliage, all CC0 1.0; their
credits are in content/licenses/polyhaven-environment.txt and ambientcg.txt.
